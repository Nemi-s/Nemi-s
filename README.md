# Flight Search — YYZ to BOM

Search for **Business Class** flights from Toronto Pearson (YYZ) to Mumbai Chhatrapati Shivaji (BOM) with up to 1 stop, for 2 adults.

## Quick Setup

```bash
pip install requests
export AMADEUS_CLIENT_ID=your_client_id
export AMADEUS_CLIENT_SECRET=your_client_secret

```

## Tips to Get Business Class at Economy-Like Prices

1. **Use miles/points** — Air Canada Aeroplan miles can book Star Alliance business class seats (Air India is Star Alliance)
2. **Bid upgrades** — Air India, Emirates and Etihad all offer bid-to-upgrade from economy
3. **Book premium economy + upgrade** — Sometimes cheaper than outright business
4. **Monitor sales** — Air India runs periodic business class sales (check their newsroom)
5. **Use credit card travel portals** — TD, RBC, and Amex travel portals sometimes have discounted business fares
